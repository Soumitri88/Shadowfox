About Sakthi Dental Clinic

Your Trusted Partner in Dental Care

Get to Know Dr. Anupriya

Bringing over 20 years of expertise, Dr. Anupriya stands as a leading figure in modern dentistry at Hosur. She began her professional journey after graduating in 2000.

Her passion for delivering exceptional dental care was further developed during six impactful years at Mathura Clinic, where she refined her clinical skills and deepened her commitment to patient wellbeing.

Our Journey

In 2004, Dr. Anupriya established Sakthi Dental Clinic in Hosur with a vision to make high-quality dental care accessible to all.

Her dedication extends beyond private practice through her long-standing service as a trusted dental consultant at the Primary Health Center at Chandara Hospital.

At Sakthi Dental Clinic, we combine advanced dental technology with a warm and patient-friendly environment to make every visit comfortable and stress-free.

From routine check-ups to specialized treatments, we focus on personalized dental care designed around the needs of every patient.

Our Mission

Our mission is to redefine oral healthcare by delivering personalized, compassionate, and advanced dental services. We are committed to creating a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable and confident in taking charge of their dental health.

By combining modern technology with patient-centric care, we aim to provide comprehensive solutions that promote long-lasting oral health.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a leading force in modern dentistry, known for setting high standards in patient care, innovation, and community engagement.

We believe in building trust, encouraging preventive dental practices, and creating a culture of excellence that makes a lasting positive impact on the community.
